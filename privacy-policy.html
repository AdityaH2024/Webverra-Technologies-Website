1. Overview & Scope

Webverra Technologies ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting any personal and project information you share with us through our website (webverra.com) and associated client engagement channels.

We collect information only when provided voluntarily by you through our consultation and project discovery forms:
