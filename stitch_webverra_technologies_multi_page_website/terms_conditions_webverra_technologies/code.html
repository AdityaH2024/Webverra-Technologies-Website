SECTION 01

Master Services Agreement & Scope Definition

By accessing the services of Webverra Technologies, executing an authorized Statement of Work (SOW), paying an onboarding deposit, or receiving development artifacts, the client (“You” or “Client”) enters into a legally binding Master Services Agreement with Webverra Technologies (“Webverra”, “we”, “us”, or “our”).

All software development, e-commerce storefront deployments, progressive web applications, WhatsApp Business API automations, and architectural consulting are executed under strict fixed-scope sprint milestones defined prior to sprint commencement.

SPRINT SCOPE CONTROL

Agile Milestone Freezes

Milestones undergo explicit acceptance criteria checks. Features outside the countersigned Scope Matrix will be queued for Subsequent Sprint Iterations.

VARIATION NOTICES

Change Request Procedure

Material changes requested mid-sprint trigger a formal written Variation Notice detailing impact on timelines, infrastructure budget, and developer hours.

SECTION 02

Intellectual Property & 100% Code Ownership

We firmly reject proprietary lock-in models. Webverra Technologies guarantees that upon complete settlement of all agreed project milestone invoices, 100% of all custom-authored source code, database architectures, graphics, and brand UI assets become the sole and exclusive intellectual property of the Client.

verified What Is Transferred Upon Milestone Completion:
  • check_circle Git Repository Handover: Full administrative ownership of GitHub, GitLab, or Bitbucket repositories with clean commit histories.
  • check_circle Figma & Vector Assets: Complete, unflattened, editable design systems, responsive wireframes, components, and brand UI kits.
  • check_circle Database Schemas & Infrastructure as Code: Supabase/PostgreSQL migration scripts, Dockerfiles, and Terraform/Cloudflare deployment manifests.
  • check_circle Open Source Tooling: All bundled open-source packages (React, Next.js, Tailwind CSS, Python libraries) remain governed under their respective MIT/Apache 2.0 permissive licenses.

Webverra Technologies retains only the non-exclusive moral right to showcase non-confidential visual interfaces in our professional portfolio and case studies, unless an explicit Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) restricting publicity has been co-executed.

SECTION 03

Milestone Payments & Invoicing Framework

To maintain accountability and predictability, Webverra operates on milestone-based billing structures. Standard builds conform to a dual-phase or multi-stage schedule depending on engagement scale:

50% STAGE 01

Initiation Retainer

Due upon countersigning the Statement of Work before development environment spin-up, UX wireframing, or architecture reservation begins.

50% STAGE 02

Deployment & Handover

Due following User Acceptance Testing (UAT) sign-off, prior to final production DNS propagation, repository transfer, and root key delivery.

Invoicing Protocols: Domestic projects in India are invoiced with applicable GST (Goods and Services Tax). International corporate clients are billed via Stripe, Wire Transfer (SWIFT/BIC), or authorized electronic escrow with invoice net terms of 7 business days unless specified otherwise.

Late payments surpassing 14 calendar days from the due date will suspend ongoing sprint activity and defer deployment dates accordingly.

SECTION 05

Post-Launch Warranty & Maintenance SLA

Webverra stands behind the resilience of its software solutions. Every standard website build, bespoke application, or e-commerce delivery includes our complimentary 30-Calendar-Day Technical Defect Warranty beginning on the live deployment date.

30-Day Defect Warranty Inclusions:

done Direct patching of functional coding bugs that depart from the signed specification matrix.
done Cross-device rendering fixes for major browsers (Chrome, Safari, iOS, Android, Firefox).
done Verification of Core Web Vitals targets on staging and initial live traffic.
done Resolution of broken checkout hooks or webhook delivery discrepancies.

Warranty Exclusions: The technical warranty does not cover issues resulting from unauthorized modification of source code by third-party contractors, breaking updates pushed by external APIs (Meta, Shopify, OpenAI), or client hosting account lapses. Post-warranty updates are available under our Monthly Retainer Agreements.

SECTION 06

Third-Party Services & Direct Account Ownership

To maintain the Client’s autonomy and protect proprietary business data, all cloud infrastructure accounts must be registered under the Client’s direct entity. Webverra Technologies does not act as a marked-up reseller of basic utility hosting or messaging quotas.

Cloud & Infrastructure: AWS, Cloudflare, Vercel, and DigitalOcean instances are linked directly to client payment credentials.

Meta / WhatsApp Business APIs: Per-conversation charges levied by Meta Platforms Inc. for WhatsApp Cloud API usage are billed straight to the Client’s Meta Business Manager.

Payment Processors: KYC verification for Razorpay, Stripe, or merchant accounts remains the strict legal responsibility of the Client corporation.

SECTION 07

Limitation of Liability & Governing Law

To the maximum extent permitted by applicable statutory law, Webverra Technologies, its directors, engineers, and affiliates shall not be liable for indirect, incidental, punitive, or consequential damages (including loss of profits, data corruption, or business interruption) arising out of the performance of web services.

In all cases, Webverra’s aggregate financial liability under any contract, tort, or negligence claim shall be strictly capped at the total amount actually paid by the Client to Webverra Technologies under the specific Statement of Work during the six (6) months immediately preceding the claim.

Governing Jurisdiction: This agreement shall be construed and governed in accordance with the laws of India. Any disputes that cannot be settled amicably within thirty (30) days shall be submitted to confidential binding arbitration under the Indian Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996, in Mumbai / jurisdictional courts of competent authority.
