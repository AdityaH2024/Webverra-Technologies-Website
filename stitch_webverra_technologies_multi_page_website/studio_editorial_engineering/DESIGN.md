## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the craft, precision, and architectural discipline of a premier digital development and e-commerce studio. The visual language rejects SaaS clichés, generic AI styling, and fluorescent glowing gradients in favor of an editorial, human, and architectural posture: warm off-white canvases, deliberate grid tension, razor-sharp typography, and restrained technical accents.

The tone communicates technical mastery and design sensibility. The primary canvas is warm, breathable, and grounded, evoking architectural portfolios and high-end editorial publications. Digital craftsmanship is signaled through realistic UI mockups, precise 1px hairline rules, and sparse, surgical applications of the signature network blue-to-purple gradient. Accents are applied strictly as precision instruments—for interactive affordances, micro-indicators, and structural nodes—never as decorative ambient haze or background blobs.

## Colors

The foundation is built on an architectural triad: warm canvas `#FAFAF8`, deep ink navy `#08111F` for supreme typographic contrast, and quiet slate `#5B6472` for editorial secondary hierarchy.

### Accent Gradient Application
The brand gradient—interpolating from `#2563EB` (Cobalt Core) to `#9333EA` (Amethyst Node) along a 135-degree axis—is strictly reserved for:
- Primary interaction triggers (buttons, key CTAs)
- Monogram glyphs and network node micro-accents
- Active status indicators and delicate focus underlines

Gradient misuse such as wide-area blurred radial backgrounds, glowing box-shadow spreads, or colored ambient washes is prohibited.

### Surface & Border Scale
- **Canvas Base:** `#FAFAF8` (warm, natural editorial base)
- **Surface Pure:** `#FFFFFF` (elevated cards, interactive panels, floating toolbars)
- **Surface Subtle:** `#F3F4F1` (subtle structural container fills and table rows)
- **Border Hairline:** `#E5E7EB` (clean, crisp architectural dividing lines)
- **Border Strong:** `#D1D5DB` (interactive state boundaries and input borders)
- **Text Primary:** `#08111F` (deep navy ink)
- **Text Secondary:** `#5B6472` (slate editorial supporting copy)
- **Text Tertiary:** `#8D96A5` (captions, metadata, structural labels)

## Typography

The type system relies entirely on Plus Jakarta Sans, selected for its balance of geometric precision and humanist warmth. It delivers clean architectural posture at scale while remaining legible across dense specs, e-commerce data displays, and technical case studies.

Headlines employ negative tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.035em`) with tight vertical proportion, generating an editorial gravitas reminiscent of modern design monographs. Body copy utilizes open line heights (`1.625` to `1.875`) to allow substantial breathing room across `#FAFAF8` surfaces. Category identifiers and studio markers use `label-caps` set in uppercase with deliberate tracking (`0.08em`) to balance technical precision against humanist body rhythm.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a 12-column responsive fluid grid constrained by a `82rem` (1312px) max-width container, anchoring the presentation with controlled margins and white space.

### Structural Spacing Rhythm
- **Micro-alignments (0.25rem - 1rem):** Internal component padding, icon-to-label inline gaps, status tags.
- **Component rhythm (1.5rem - 2rem):** Form groups, card internal padding, list separations.
- **Section rhythm (4.5rem - 9rem):** Generous inter-section intervals that preserve breathing room and focus on showcased artifacts.

### Responsive Behavior
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns, 1.5rem (24px) gutters, 3rem (48px) page margins. Asymmetrical column pairings (e.g., 5-column editorial sticky sidebars opposite 7-column project viewports).
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, 1.25rem (20px) gutters, 2rem (32px) margins. Columns collapse to 4/4 splits.
- **Mobile (under 768px):** 4 columns, 1rem (16px) gutters, 1.25rem (20px) margins. Multi-column cards stack vertically into clean sequential flows.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses flat architectural planes, crisp 1px borders, and ultra-diffused, low-opacity ambient shadows. Heavy dropshadows, colored glow fields, and fuzzy halos are omitted.

### Depth Hierarchy
1. **Base Layer (Elevation 0):** `#FAFAF8` canvas with no shadow. Structural demarcations rely purely on `1px solid #E5E7EB` borders.
2. **Card & Surface Plane (Elevation 1):** Crisp `#FFFFFF` surface with `1px solid #E5E7EB` and subtle multi-layered ambient depth:
   - `box-shadow: 0 1px 2px rgba(8, 17, 31, 0.03), 0 4px 12px rgba(8, 17, 31, 0.02);`
3. **Floating Overlays & Menus (Elevation 2):** Pure `#FFFFFF` surface with hairline perimeter:
   - `box-shadow: 0 4px 6px -1px rgba(8, 17, 31, 0.04), 0 12px 24px -4px rgba(8, 17, 31, 0.06);`
4. **Interactive Hover State:** Elements elevate subtly on the Y-axis by 2px with an expanded, low-opacity spread:
   - `transform: translateY(-2px);`
   - `box-shadow: 0 8px 20px -4px rgba(8, 17, 31, 0.08);`
   - Border transitions to `#D1D5DB`.

### Browser & Device Mockup Chrome
Showcased client builds and e-commerce interfaces sit inside realistic, minimal browser chrome: pure white framing, razor-thin `1px solid #E5E7EB` edge lines, minimal traffic light controls (`#E5E7EB` or muted charcoal dots), and light grey simulated address bars (`#F3F4F1`).

## Shapes

The design system adopts a soft, disciplined geometry (Level 1 roundedness). This avoids both harsh brutalist square corners and playful, overly bubbly pill shapes, maintaining an editorial, high-end studio presence.

- **Base Radius (`0.25rem` / 4px):** Form fields, badges, code tags, tab indicators.
- **Standard Card Radius (`0.5rem` / 8px):** Portfolio tiles, service cards, content modals, browser mockup chrome.
- **Large Container Radius (`0.75rem` / 12px):** Hero showcase panels, framed studio case viewports.
- **Pill Variant (`9999px`):** Reserved exclusively for live network telemetry badges, filter chips, and circular icon nodes.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Gradient Action:** Fill gradient `linear-gradient(135deg, #2563EB 0%, #9333EA 100%)`, text `#FFFFFF`, radius `0.25rem`, padding `0.75rem 1.5rem`, font `label-md`. Subtle hover brightness filter (1.05) and smooth `translateY(-1px)`.
- **Secondary Studio Button:** Fill `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E5E7EB`, text `#08111F`. On hover: border `#08111F`, background `#FAFAF8`.
- **Ghost/Tertiary:** Fill transparent, text `#08111F`, font `label-md`. On hover: text `#2563EB` with an arrow glyph shifting 3px right.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Badges:** Height 24px, radius `9999px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E5E7EB`, padding `0 0.625rem`. Houses a 6px circular node indicator with the brand blue-to-purple gradient, followed by `label-caps` in `#5B6472`.
- **Category Filter Chips:** Radius `0.25rem`, background `#F3F4F1`, text `#5B6472`. Active state transitions to fill `#08111F`, text `#FFFFFF`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- **Inputs:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E5E7EB`, radius `0.25rem`, padding `0.75rem 1rem`, typography `body-md` in `#08111F`. Placeholder `#8D96A5`.
- **Focus State:** Border `#2563EB`, outer ring `2px solid rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.15)`. No dark heavy outlines.
- **Labels:** Positioned above input, typography `label-md`, color `#08111F`, gap `0.375rem`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Boxes:** Dimensions 18x18px, border `1.5px solid #D1D5DB`, radius `3px`, background `#FFFFFF`.
- **Selected State:** Fill gradient `linear-gradient(135deg, #2563EB 0%, #9333EA 100%)`, border transparent, crisp white 1.5px checkmark icon.

### Cards & Project Viewports
- **Case Study Cards:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E5E7EB`, radius `0.5rem`, padding `1.5rem`. Structured with an image aspect ratio of 16:10 for e-commerce and web applications, hairline divider, followed by an editorial typographic footer pairing client sector (`label-caps`) and title (`headline-sm`).
- **Browser Mockup Shells:** Clean header bar with three 8px light neutral dots, clean URL capsule in `#FAFAF8`, displaying full-bleed interactive client previews.

### Lists & Key Deliverables
- Clean horizontal rules (`1px solid #E5E7EB`) between items.
- Numeric counters rendered in `label-caps` in `#8D96A5` (e.g., `01`, `02`, `03`) preceding the item title.
