What Affects Your Project Price?

We believe an educated partner makes the best decisions. Here is exactly what moves an estimate up or down before contract finalization.

Project Scope & Page Count

Custom Calculators & Logic

Third-Party Integrations

Connecting custom web forms directly into Hubspot, Zoho CRM, ERP databases, or warehouse shipping fulfillment requires secure API middleware and webhook authentication.

Providing high-resolution brand photography, product shots, and finalized copywriting reduces development hours significantly compared to builds requiring complete asset creation.

TERMS & GOVERNANCE

Clear Terms, Fair Working Relationships

No murky contracts or convoluted retainer clauses. We pride ourselves on business-friendly transparency.

payments What is the standard milestone payment schedule?

We work on a straightforward 50/50 milestone structure. A 50% kick-off deposit initializes the sprint, UX design, and architecture phase. The remaining 50% balance is only invoiced once the project is fully constructed on our staging environment, thoroughly tested, and granted final pre-launch approval by you.

receipt Do you provide GST invoices and international billing?

Yes. For clients based in India, we issue fully compliant domestic GST invoices with complete input tax credit documentation. For international partners across the US, UK, Middle East, and APAC, we provide electronic SWIFT wire billing and secure Stripe gateway invoicing in your native currency.

lock_open Do we own the source code and assets after completion?

Absolutely, 100%. Unlike agencies that hold client databases hostage behind proprietary subscription systems, we hand over full administrative ownership, repository access, and credentials the minute final settlement is concluded. Your software is your company's intellectual property.

domain_verification Are domain, hosting, and third-party API fees included?

Our quotes cover all professional engineering, UX design, code integration, and system optimization. Hosting subscriptions (e.g., Vercel, AWS, Cloudflare) and external SaaS licenses (e.g., Meta Cloud WhatsApp API, payment gateway fees) are purchased directly in your name so you retain direct account ownership.

handshake What happens if scope changes during development?

If your team requires additional functionality or new third-party integrations outside the initial scope document, we evaluate the addition together. We provide a separate, written micro-estimate before any extra work begins. You will never see an unexpected invoice item.
