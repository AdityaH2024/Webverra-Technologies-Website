06 Retention & Shielding

Data Retention & Security Protocols

Client project files and interaction histories are stored in access-restricted datacenters with SOC2 Type II accreditation.

In-Transit Encryption

All communications enforced via TLS 1.3 cryptographic tunnels with HSTS preloading.

At-Rest Protection

Stored database backups and audit records secured under AES-256 bit military-grade keys.

Statutory transaction and invoice records are preserved for a period of up to 7 (seven) financial years to satisfy corporate tax, GST, and foreign remittance audit requirements, after which they are permanently expunged.
