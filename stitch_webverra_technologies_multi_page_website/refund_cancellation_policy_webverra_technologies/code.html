Project Cancellation Guidelines

Webverra Technologies operates as an elite digital engineering firm. We recognize that strategic priorities, market circumstances, or internal client directives may pivot during contract initiation. We provide transparent exit frameworks tailored to each development phase.

schedule

Discovery & Architectural Phase

If cancellation is requested in writing prior to the commencement of active code sprint development (during requirement specification, schema mapping, or initial wireframing), clients are eligible for an immediate settlement review with minimal deductions.

Should both parties mutually identify insurmountable regulatory, technical, or licensing impediments preventing scope delivery, either party may invoke amicable dissolution without punitive contractual fees.

Advance Deposits & Milestone Policy

Custom software engineering requires upfront reservation of senior full-stack developers, cloud architects, and UI/UX specialists. Our financial milestone model operates on verifiable deliverables and fair reciprocity.

50% Kickoff Advance Deposit

Initial project deposit reserves designated sprint engineering bandwidth, initiates repository provisioning, architecture blueprints, and design system setup.

Pro-Rata Eligible

Accepted Milestone Deliverables

Once a project milestone (e.g., Staging Environment Alpha, Completed Checkout Flow, or Production Deployment) is formally reviewed, demonstrated, and accepted in writing or staging approval, the corresponding milestone fee becomes non-refundable.

Completed Sprints Final

Transparent Pro-Rata Assessment

If mid-sprint cancellation occurs before a milestone reaches completion, the unexpended portion of the advance deposit is refunded based strictly on logged engineering and architectural hours validated through Git commits and task timesheets.

05

Resolution Process & Refund Timelines

Every billing query or cancellation is managed by an assigned Senior Partner and our finance desk. Below is our statutory timeline:

1

Formal Notice Submission

Submit cancellation request with Project ID to contact@webverra.com. An automated compliance ticket is instantly generated.

2

Engineering & Timesheet Audit (3 Business Days)

Our lead architect and billing controller cross-reference logged work, repository commits, and completed deliverables to calculate the fair pro-rata disbursement.

3

Disbursement Execution (5–7 Banking Days)

Approved refund amounts are automatically transmitted directly to the original payment method:

  • Credit/Debit Card via Stripe: 5 to 7 business days depending on issuing bank.
  • PayPal Balance / Bank Transfer: 3 to 5 business days.
  • Domestic UPI / Net Banking via Razorpay: 5 to 7 working banking days.
  • International SWIFT Wire Transfer: 7 to 10 international clearing days.
lock For anti-money laundering (AML) compliance, refunds cannot be redirected to third-party bank accounts or alternative credit cards.
